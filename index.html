Module 1

Course IntroductionThe Core Roles and Responsibilities of an English Language Teacher

Overview

English language teachers play a central role in helping learners develop language competence, communicative competence, and the ability to use English appropriately for academic and real-world purposes. In modern language education, a teacher's task is not limited to transmitting grammar and vocabulary; it also includes designing learning activities, guiding learning strategies, assessing outcomes, and building a positive classroom environment.

Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students should be able to:

  • Explain the twelve core roles and responsibilities of an English language teacher.
  • Understand and use essential ELT terminology accurately.
  • Analyse classroom situations and propose appropriate teaching strategies.
  • Apply pedagogical knowledge to realistic teaching scenarios.
  • Evaluate their own understanding through interactive exercises.

Study Time & Format

Estimated study time: 25–35 minutes. Includes reading, quick-check activities, an assessment, and reflection. You may revisit the material as often as needed.

Why understanding teacher roles matters

Understanding teacher roles helps future teachers make principled pedagogical decisions, allocate time sensibly, and avoid reducing instruction to a single skill. A teacher's role is closely tied to decisions about content, method, assessment, and learner support — all of which directly influence student progress.
